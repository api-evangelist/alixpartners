The return of geopolitics: A look at how geopolitics is impacting markets, the global economy, and business strategy. As tensions between the US and China continue and the war in Ukraine enters its second year, AlixPartners CEO Simon Freakley and former Editor-In-Chief of The Wall Street Journal, Matt Murray, explore the extent to which geopolitics is impacting markets, the global economy, and business strategy. Moderated by William Lewis, former CEO of Dow Jones, the conversation takes a look at how business leaders are navigating the current state of play.
