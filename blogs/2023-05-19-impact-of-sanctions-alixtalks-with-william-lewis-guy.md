As global sanctions against Russia, in response to its invasion of Ukraine, continue to mount at an unprecedented scale and scope, the regulatory and stakeholder risks associated with a misstep have never been higher. In this AlixTalks session, AlixPartners CEO Simon Freakley opens a discussion on the topic led by William Lewis, former CEO of Dow Jones and Publisher of The Wall Street Journal, with Guy Harrison, CEO of SmartSearch, Tom Scampion, Managing Director at AlixPartners, and Wendy Wysong, Partner at Steptoe.
